Classroom Wrap-Up & Discussion

Lecturer's Retail Value Chain Takeaways

🔗

1. Interdependency of Links

Flashy Marketing & Sales campaigns fail if Inbound Logistics leaves shelves empty—wasting ad spend and destroying customer trust (NPS).

📊

2. Where Margin Is Won

Retail operates on razor-thin net margins. Competitive advantage stems from tight alignment between Procurement, RFID Inventory Tech, and lean Store Operations.

omnichannel 🏪📱

3. Omnichannel Fulfillment

Modern retail stores act as hybrid micro-fulfillment hubs where Outbound Logistics (BOPIS, same-day delivery) and frictionless Service drive repeat visits.
